Use case 2: One contact, many debtors

Debt agreement proposals are lodged one at a time, with many different administrators. When the same contact details appear on unrelated debtors' proposals, someone else may be arranging them. This runs on proposal forms and outcomes the regulator already receives, without waiting for a complaint.
