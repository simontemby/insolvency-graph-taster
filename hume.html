Hume

This site is one case, worked out in advance on a small synthetic graph. A regulator needs the same reasoning on live data from its own systems, used by analysts who don't write queries. GraphAware Hume is built for that work.

GraphAware describes Hume as "a graph-powered intelligence analysis platform for mission-critical environments". On 3 June 2026, Neo4j announced an agreement to acquire GraphAware.

Sources: GraphAware, Hume · Neo4j press release, 3 June 2026

Deciding who is who

TasterContext came from one hand-set rule: an identifier shared by more than eight entities is ignored. On this synthetic test, that rule took precision from 61% to 95%.

HumeHume resolves entities in two ways: automatically, in Orchestra pipelines as data is loaded, and by analysts, who can group records they judge to be the same person or company into one entity. Neo4j, Hume
