What builds up

A file handled on its own is finished when it closes. A connected record keeps working: each proposal lodged joins what is already known, so the next one arrives with more around it.

A first step

None of this needs new data to begin. A first step can be small: bring together the records already received (proposal forms, lodgements, complaints, the registers and the NPII) and run it alongside the current queue for a quarter.

Then compare what it surfaces with what the queue found on its own. That comparison is the real test, on real records, and it is the one this site cannot run.

Every claim on this site was tested on data with known answers, and its limits are stated.
